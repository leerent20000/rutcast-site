Rutcast support

Deer and moose hunting maps for Ontario, for iPhone

Questions, bug reports or suggestions: email ross-leerentveld@outlook.com. Replies usually within a few days.

Hunt Groups

Hunt Groups need every member to be signed in to iCloud on their iPhone, with iCloud Drive turned on. If an invite link won't open in Rutcast, update the app and check those two settings, then tap the link again.

To see members in the background, allow location "Always" in Settings > Rutcast > Location.

Helpful details to include

Your iPhone model, iOS version, the Rutcast version (shown in Settings), and a screenshot if something looks wrong.

Privacy

Rutcast has no account and the developer receives none of your information. Read the privacy policy, or go back to the Rutcast home page.
