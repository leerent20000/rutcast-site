Rutcast privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Rutcast has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. Your waypoints, tracks and journal stay on your iPhone. If you join a hunt group, your name, position, facing direction and battery level are shared with that group through your own iCloud account, only while you choose to share. To show weather and maps, Rutcast sends the location of the area you are looking at to Canadian government data services, to OpenStreetMap's Overpass service and to Apple.

What Rutcast stores on your iPhone

Waypoints, recorded GPS tracks, drawings, journal entries and sightings, photos you attach, watched spots for movement forecasts, downloaded offline map areas, and your settings.

This information is stored only on your device and is not uploaded by Rutcast. It may be included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer), according to your device settings. Deleting Rutcast deletes everything it stores on your iPhone.

Hunt Groups

Hunt Groups are optional. When you create or join a group, Rutcast uses Apple's CloudKit to store the group in the iCloud account of the person who created it, shared only with the people they invite.

While you are sharing, the other members of that group can see:

  • the display name you choose in Rutcast,
  • your latest position, accuracy and the direction your phone is facing,
  • your phone's battery level, and when you last updated,
  • stands, blinds and other points you add to the group.

Rutcast keeps only your latest position in a group, not a history of where you have been. Sharing starts only when you turn it on and stops on its own after the time you choose, or when you stop it.

Group data is held by Apple in iCloud under Apple's privacy policy. The developer of Rutcast has no access to it.

Location

With your permission, Rutcast uses your location to show where you are on the map, point the wind arrow the way you are facing, give weather and forecasts for where you are, and record tracks.

If you allow "Always", Rutcast uses your location in the background only while you are recording a track or sharing your position with a hunt group, so these keep working with the phone in your pocket. Background use stops when the recording or sharing stops.

You can change or withdraw location permission at any time in the Settings app.

Camera and photos

Rutcast uses the camera or your photo library only when you choose to attach a photo to a waypoint or journal entry. Attached photos are copied into Rutcast's own storage on your device and are not uploaded or shared with your group.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, forecast alerts for spots you watch are scheduled on your iPhone. No notification service of the developer's is involved.

What else is sent off your iPhone

To fetch weather, wind and forecasts, Rutcast sends a coordinate, or the edges of the map area on screen, to Environment and Climate Change Canada (weather.gc.ca).

To draw map layers such as Wildlife Management Units, Crown land, roads, slope and topographic maps, Rutcast sends the edges of the map area on screen to Land Information Ontario and to Government of Canada mapping services.

To find Hunt Spots, Rutcast sends the edges of the map area on screen to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass API (overpass-api.de, operated in Germany, or a public mirror if it is unavailable) to get road and trail data. Results are cached on your iPhone for 30 days so the same area is not requested again.

The base map and place search are provided by Apple Maps, under Apple's privacy policy.

These requests contain no name, account, device identifier or other information about you. Like any internet request, they reveal your device's IP address to the service that answers them. Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy.

What Rutcast does not do

Rutcast does not require an account, use analytics or advertising, track you across apps or websites, or sell your data. The developer does not receive any of your information.

Exports and backups you make

When you export a backup, GPX or KML file, Rutcast writes it where you choose. What happens to it after that is up to you.

Children

Rutcast is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
